ImageCast

Find an image. Put it on the big screen.

  1. Click Open display. A window opens on your HDMI screen. Click it once to go fullscreen.
  2. Search for anything. Click a picture and it appears on the big screen.
  3. Use the arrow keys to step through results. B blacks out the screen.

Found something on Google, Bing or anywhere else? Drag the picture into this window, or copy it and press ⌘V / Ctrl+V.
